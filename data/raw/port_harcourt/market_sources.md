# Port Harcourt market research source / search / discard log

**Research date:** 2026-10-02. **Method:** native web search/open only; no Firecrawl. Search language: English. This log records the main searches, URLs used, and evidence decisions for the memo.

## Search batches and outcomes

### 1. Population, economy, oil and construction

Searches:

- `Port Harcourt Nigeria population 2024 2025 official statistics Rivers State population NBS`
- `Port Harcourt Nigeria oil gas hub official Rivers State investment construction economy`
- `Rivers State 2025 budget infrastructure housing Port Harcourt ring road official`
- `Nigeria inflation August 2026 NBS headline CPI`

Used:

- [NBS/NPC Population Forecasts](https://nigerianstat.gov.ng/pdfuploads/Population%20Forecasts.pdf) — official state forecast; old enough that it is not treated as a 2026 city population.
- [BusinessDay Ring Road/population feature PDF](https://cdn.businessday.ng/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/BD_20240414.pdf) — 2022 projections for Port Harcourt City, Obio/Akpor and Rivers; used with boundary/source caveat.
- [NBS IGR 2023](https://www.nigerianstat.gov.ng/elibrary/read/1241579) — official Rivers IGR figure.
- [NIPC Rivers profile](https://nipc.gov.ng/nigeria/rivers) — investment/oil/port context; headline population/GDP claims treated as directional because the calculation is not transparent.
- [RSIPA sectors](https://rsipa.riversstateapps.ng/sectors/) — official state investment positioning; not used as an independent market-size estimate.
- [NUPRC 2024 Annual Report](https://www.nuprc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/UPDATED-NUPRC-2024-ANNUAL-REPORT-1.pdf) — Rivers crude/gas attribution.
- [NNPC 2024 refinery statement](https://www.nnpcgroup.com/insights/nnpc-ltd-reaffirms-progress-on-port-harcourt-refinery-and-addresses-misleading-claims), [NMDPRA 2025 fact sheet](https://alps.blob.core.windows.net/nmdprawebsite/Statistics/Upload-80c82709-7f84-4cea-959e-668d6e6d030e.pdf), [NNPC 2026 refinery MoU](https://www.nnpcgroup.com/insights/press-release-nnpc-chinese-firms-sign-mo-u-towards-restart-expansion-port-harcourt-refineries) — used chronologically to distinguish restart claim, shutdown and proposed restart/expansion.
- [Julius Berger Ring Road release](https://www.julius-berger.com/fileadmin/julius_berger_nigeria_pictures/7_0_press/press_releases/news/2023.06.19_news_release_ring_road_ph.pdf) — verified infrastructure scale.
- [Rivers 2025 budget PDF](https://www.riversstate.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/RIVERS-State-FY-2025-Budget-Publication-1-1.pdf) and [Rivers 2026 budget statement](https://moic.rv.gov.ng/rivers-state-2026-budget-statement-presented-to-the-rivers-state-house-of-assembly/) — budget pipeline signals only.
- [Premium Times report of NBS August 2026 CPI](https://www.premiumtimesng.com/news/headlines/909793-nigeria-inflation-falls-to-15-39-in-august-nbs.html) — secondary report of current NBS inflation; used only for macro constraint.
- [NBS current indicators](https://www.nigerianstat.gov.ng/index.php?lbw=1) — Q1 2026 national GDP/construction indicators.

Discarded or limited:

- Wikipedia city/state pages — not used for core population/economic claims where NBS/BusinessDay sources were available.
- NIPC’s 7.5m/₦21tn spotlight figures — not used as hard facts because the underlying calculation is not transparent and the boundary differs from other sources.
- Unverified current refinery tracker pages — not used against NNPC/NMDPRA primary sources.

### 4. Logistics

Searches:

- `Lagos to Port Harcourt road distance driving time route map`
- `Lekki Lagos to Port Harcourt driving distance`
- `Lagos Port Harcourt freight trucking delivery Nigeria cargo service`
- `Port Harcourt warehouse storage logistics Trans Amadi Onne last mile`
- `East West Road Port Harcourt traffic condition freight 2025 official`

Used:

- [Stone Depot address and service](https://www.stonedepotng.com/) — current origin address and fabrication/distribution positioning.
- [AFRoute](https://afroute.com/ng/lagos/port-harcourt), [DistancesFrom](https://www.distancesfrom.com/ng/directions-from-Lagos-to-Port-Harcourt/DirectionHistory/249619.aspx), [Travelmath](https://www.travelmath.com/driving-time/from/Lagos,+Nigeria/to/Port+Harcourt,+Nigeria) — route-distance/time range; not treated as a freight SLA.
- [GoFlow](https://goflow.com.ng/freight), [Travo](https://services.travo.ng/haulage-services/), [HaulConnect](https://www.haulconnect.com.ng/) — public freight transit-time/service options; prices generally require a quote.
- [BtSh Lagos–PH corridor](https://btsh.africa/corridor/lagos-portharcourt) — route distance, cargo categories, truck types, GPS/escrow model.
- [Flagship](https://www.flagshipnigeria.com/) — one published route-rate methodology; used as a benchmark only, not a Cosentino slab quote.
- [Trans Amadi Logistics](https://www.transamadilogistics.com/) and [Quicka](https://quickaltd.com/) — potential local warehousing/haulage vendors; self-reported claims require audit.
- [NPA Onne](https://nigerianports.gov.ng/onne/), [OGFZA Onne](https://ogfza.gov.ng/free-zones/onne/), [Hapag-Lloyd Inland Nigeria](https://www.hapag-lloyd.com/content/dam/website/downloads/pdf/24380_Flyer_Inland_Nigeria_Updated.pdf) — port/warehouse/inland-delivery options, mainly relevant to future direct/import/project logistics.
- [Port Harcourt freight-corridor study](https://doi.org/10.15580/gjss.2025.1.032425044) and [Vanguard 2026 traffic report](https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/02/motorists-decry-gridlock-on-lagos-ibadan-sagamu-benin-highways/amp/) — congestion/safety/road-risk evidence.

Discarded or limited:

- Passenger-bus fares and parcel prices — not applicable to full-size slabs.
- Exact “Lekki to Port Harcourt” distance from generic city calculators — used only to infer a 620–650 km planning range from the sourced Lagos route; carrier quote must control.
- No public, slab-specific, insured, all-in freight quote was found. The ₦2–4m dedicated-load range in the memo is explicitly a planning estimate anchored by the public Flagship methodology and to be replaced by three written carrier quotes.

## Final evidence gaps

- No public 2026 Port Harcourt premium-surface market size, annual m² consumption or complete fabricator census.
- No independently verified current Caesarstone PH showroom address/equipment audit.
- No current public PH distributor evidence for Quartzforms, Compac, Neolith, Laminam or Vicostone.
- No named decision-maker reliably published for the shortlisted developers/studios.
- No carrier quote specific to Stone Depot’s actual crate count, slab weight, vehicle, insurance and delivery point.
